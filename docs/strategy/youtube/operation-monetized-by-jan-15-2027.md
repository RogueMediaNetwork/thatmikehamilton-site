## Major Issues to Fix

### 1. Long-form retention is the biggest problem

For **Texas Just Paused AI Data Centers**, the long-form video was 14:46.

- Average view duration: about **4:03**
- Average percentage viewed: about **27.45%**

The retention curve shows major early drop-off. The issue is not simply video length; the opening and story progression need to earn more time.

### 2. Openings need to get to the story immediately

Avoid:

- greetings
- biography
- "today we're going to talk about..."
- long setup before stakes

Use this structure:

**CHANGE / PROBLEM -> STAKES -> CURIOSITY -> PROMISE -> PROOF -> STORY**

Example:

> Texas is approving billions of dollars worth of AI infrastructure — but the companies building it all need something the state is already struggling to provide: electricity.

Then:

> And if they're wrong about how much power they'll need, this stops being a tech-industry problem. It becomes your power-bill problem.

Then:

> So I went through what's actually being built, how much electricity it needs, and who's ultimately going to pay for it.

### 3. Videos are not chaining into each other

Traffic source data showed:

- Shorts feed: **4,474 views**
- YouTube Search: **101**
- Related/Suggested: **51**
- End screens: **1**

That means the channel is publishing isolated videos instead of creating viewing sessions.

Fix every long-form video with:

- spoken next-video CTA
- end screen to the next logical video
- pinned comment to the next logical video
- description link to the next logical video
- playlist placement

### 4. Shorts are getting views but not enough subscribers

Recent 12-month analytics:

- Shorts views: **4,807**
- Subscribers gained from Shorts: about **3**

That is very weak subscriber conversion.

Shorts need to do more than get views. Their job is:

**Hook stranger -> establish Mike's worldview -> point toward a deeper story**

Use a specific continuation CTA rather than a generic "like and subscribe."

Example:

> The electricity problem is actually much bigger than this. I broke down what Texas is facing in the full video.

## Long-Form Format

Target length for now:

**12–16 minutes**

Do not deliberately aim for 19–25 minutes until retention justifies it.

### Opening Template

**0:00–0:15 — Event / change**

Immediate concrete event or tension.

**0:15–0:30 — Stakes**

Explain why the stranger should care.

**0:30–0:45 — Promise**

Tell the viewer what they will understand by staying.

Then move into story.

### Retention Rule

Every **60–90 seconds**, create a genuine reason to continue.

Examples:

- But there's a problem.
- And this is where Texas enters the story.
- That number sounds enormous until you see what comes next.
- The jobs aren't where most people think they are.
- There's one piece of this almost nobody is talking about.

Do not use fake cliffhangers. Use genuine unanswered questions and escalating stakes.

## Shorts Format

Each long-form should generate **2–4 Shorts**, but not random excerpts.

Each Short should be rewritten as a self-contained story:

- **0–2 sec:** startling proposition
- **2–10 sec:** context
- **10–30 sec:** reveal
- **30–45 sec:** consequence
- **final line:** connect to deeper/full story

Current data suggests **30–60 seconds** is a strong working range.
