# Channel Portfolio & Current Experiments

## Objective

Build a small portfolio of differentiated channels and use evidence-driven long-form publishing to pursue YPP monetization before the January 2027 internal deadline.

Do not activate every available channel at once. Concentrate production on the strongest hypotheses and double down when a topic materially exceeds baseline.

## 2. Rogue Music — Priority 2

Current subscribers observed: 143.

Previous positioning:
- deep work / focus music

New experiment:
> Music lists with a story.

Do not simply upload copyrighted greatest-hits compilations.

Format:
- 12–20 minute original narrated countdowns
- music history
- artist stories
- song origins
- rankings
- cultural/music-business context

Examples:
- 10 Famous Songs With Stories Darker Than the Lyrics
- 10 Huge '80s Songs the Artists Actually Hated
- 10 One-Hit Wonders That Made an Absolute Fortune

Current vidIQ evidence:
- A recent video titled "Top 10 Saddest Stories Behind Rock's Most Iconic Songs" from a channel with roughly 2.7K subscribers had roughly 396K views and ~2.6K views/hour when researched.
- Other small-channel list/history examples were also reaching tens or hundreds of thousands of views.

Production advantage:
- cheaper/faster than cinematic AI film
- natural retention structure through countdown
- can use narration, graphics, licensed/appropriate imagery, headlines, charts, motion graphics and owned background music

Rights rule:
- do not depend on long copyrighted song excerpts or unlicensed music compilations.

## Portfolio Rule

The channels may share infrastructure, but must not share a generic creative identity.

Shared:
- research engine
- model adapters
- render queue
- QC
- timeline tooling
- analytics infrastructure

Distinct per channel:
- editorial promise
- voice
- pacing
- title grammar
- thumbnail grammar
- visual style
- narrator/personality
- topic universe

## Breakout Rule

When one topic achieves approximately 3–5x the channel's normal performance, immediately research and prepare 3–5 adjacent videos while demand is demonstrated.
